The Full-Paper Imitation Laboratory

Nine real open-access papers. Three intensive studio days. Eleven major sections. Your group will locate the moves that published researchers make, test those moves, and rebuild its own paper section by section.

The rule: imitate the move, never copy the sentence.

A published paper is a craft model, not an authority that is correct in every detail. Every task follows the same cycle: find → name the move → judge it → rebuild it → apply it → verify it. Keep the model PDF open beside your group draft.

Your three-day route

Each day ends with finished text that belongs in the final group paper. Abstract analysis happens early, but the abstract is written last.

SUN · 100 min
Build the argument’s front half
Whole-paper anatomy → title → introduction funnel → research-question alignment → literature-review synthesis.
5 revised artifacts
MON · 100 min
Turn procedure and evidence into claims
Required common reading → methodology reproducibility → results/data analysis → seven-move discussion → limitations.

Conclusion → traceable recommendations → abstract written last → two-way reference audit → whole-paper coherence audit → editor check.

Which groups start with which papers?

The tool need not match exactly. Match the research problem, variables, participants, instruments, or analytical design.

Writing feedback + grammar

Groups 1 and 8: begin with Papers 01, 05, and 06. Add Paper 03 for grammar and self-correction measures.

Pronunciation + speaking tools

Groups 2, 3, 4, 6, and 10: begin with Papers 02 and 04. Add Paper 07 for conversational AI, fluency, and anxiety.

Speaking + anxiety

Group 5: begin with Paper 07, then compare how Papers 02 and 04 operationalize speaking performance.

Reading + vocabulary

Groups 7 and 9: begin with Papers 09 and 08. Add Paper 03 for vocabulary measurement and a deliberately critical comparison.

Section-to-paper imitation map

Do not read every paper from page 1. Start with the section you are repairing, inspect one primary model and one comparison model, then rebuild the move in your own words and evidence.

Paper sectionStart withMove to huntYour imitation product
TitlePapers 06 + 09Tool/intervention, outcome, population, and context without an exaggerated causal promise.One precise title that matches the actual design.
AbstractPapers 01 + 06 + 09Problem → purpose → method → main result → warranted implication.Five-move abstract, drafted only after the paper is stable.
Introduction + RQsPapers 02 + 03 + 09Local context → known evidence → tension/gap → significance → purpose and ordered RQs.Five-sentence funnel plus an RQ alignment chain.
Literature reviewPapers 01 + 05 + 07Sources placed in agreement, extension, contrast, or unresolved tension—not one summary per paragraph.One synthesis paragraph organized by relationship.
MethodologyPapers 01 + 02 + 04Design, participants, instrument, procedure, analysis, ethics, and reliability described reproducibly.Past-tense account of what actually happened.
Data analysis + resultsPapers 03 + 04 + 09Results ordered by RQ; tables and prose report evidence without explaining causes too early.One RQ-linked results paragraph and a clean table callout.
DiscussionPapers 01 + 05 + 07Finding → interpretation → literature comparison → explanation → qualification → implication.One seven-move paragraph with calibrated claims.
Conclusion + recommendationsPapers 02 + 06 + 09Answer the RQs, state the bounded contribution, then trace recommendations backward to evidence.Four-move conclusion plus two evidence-to-action chains.
Reference listPapers 01 + 04 + 09Consistent author/year matching, sentence case, journal and volume italics, and DOI format.Two-way text-to-list and list-to-text reconciliation.
Imitation protocol: copy no sentence. Record the move in plain language, identify why it works, rebuild it with your study’s evidence, and ask the skeptic to verify that the new claim does not exceed the design.

Before Sunday: ten-minute setup

  1. Download your three assigned PDFs to the group laptop or open the hosted copies.
  2. Open the latest complete group draft—not isolated section files.
  3. Choose four rotating roles: PDF navigator, move hunter, skeptic, and group scribe.
  4. Bring the approved research questions, final tables/results, and current reference list.

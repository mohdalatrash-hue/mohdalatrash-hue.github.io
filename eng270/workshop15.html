Week 15 workshop · Consolidation

Agreements, Contracts and the Whole Course

The last genre and the last workshop: contracts, peace agreements and Security Council language — then a pass back across all fifteen weeks.

🎯 What this workshop trains

The final exam has a theoretical half and a practical half. This workshop rehearses both: the named concepts from every module, and one last piece of precision translation.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 32: Agreements and contracts (pp. 194–199), Lesson 33: Security Council terms and peace agreements (pp. 200–205), and the Module 7 review (p. 206).
🔍 One last pass across the course. Each item names a decision from an earlier week. Can you still place it?
✍️ Two final tasks. One institutional, one contractual. Terminology list first, then translate, then check your modality.
📝 Final review — 14 questions drawn from the whole course, weighted towards the named concepts the theoretical half of the final exam tests.

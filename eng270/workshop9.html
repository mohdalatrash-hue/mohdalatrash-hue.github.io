Week 9 workshop · Practical session · scientific texts

Equivalence, Level by Level

Baker’s three levels — word, above word, and text — applied to scientific prose, plus the Arabic inflection that catches every student out.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Weeks 2–4 gave you methods for whole texts. This workshop gives you the diagnostic vocabulary for the level at which a particular difficulty actually sits — which is what turns ‘this is hard’ into a solvable problem.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 21 (pp. 98–100), Lesson 22 (pp. 101–108), Lesson 23 (pp. 109–114), Lesson 24 (pp. 115–119).
🔍 Name the level. Word level, above word level, or textual level — and then the specific problem.
✍️ Two passages, two levels. The first tests word- and above-word-level equivalence; the second tests whether your Arabic holds together as a text.
📝 Week 9 review — 10 questions on equivalence at word, phrase and text level.

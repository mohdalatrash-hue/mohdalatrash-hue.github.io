Week 4 workshop · Foundation methods and problems

Method or Procedure?

Faithful, communicative and adaptive translation, the whole-text / sentence-level distinction, and the four families every later difficulty belongs to.

🎯 What this workshop trains

This is the diagnostic week. By the end of it you should be able to look at any difficulty in Weeks 5–15 and say which family it belongs to — because naming the family is usually most of the solution.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lessons 3–5 (pp. 9–17) and the Module 1 review (p. 20); Ghazala (2012) on translation problems.
🔍 Sort the difficulty. Name the family — grammatical, lexical, stylistic or phonological — or the method at work.
✍️ One sentence, three methods. Translate each item faithfully, then communicatively, and say which you would submit for which reader.
📝 Week 4 review — 10 questions on the foundation methods and the four problem families.

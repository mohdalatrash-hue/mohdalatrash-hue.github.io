Week 4 workshop · Foundation methods and problems

Method or Procedure?

Faithful, communicative and adaptive translation, the whole-text / sentence-level distinction, and the four families every later difficulty belongs to.

🎯 What this workshop trains

This is the diagnostic week. Work through all four stations in order: recall the concepts, diagnose the difficulty, produce translations, and finish with the cumulative quiz. Naming the family begins the solution; context, purpose and readership complete it.

📖 Station 1 · 14 concept checks. Use the explanations after every answer as a compact review. Reading: Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lessons 3–5 (pp. 9–17); Ghazala (2012), pp. 17–27.
🔍 Station 2 · 16 diagnostic cases. Name the method or problem family, then explain what is at stake.
✍️ Station 3 · 10 translation tasks. Write before revealing the model notes. More than one answer may be defensible if your reasoning fits the context.
📝 Station 4 · 25-question final quiz. Immediate feedback explains every answer. Aim for 80% or higher.
New evidence workshop: Work through 30 real public-domain excerpts and instructional Arabic translations. Identify the method or translation problem, then defend your answer. Open Excerpt Detective →

Week 10 workshop · Practical session · media texts

Leads and Headlines

Front-loaded, plain, active and short. What media language does, and how a headline gets translated as a headline rather than expanded into a sentence.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Media translation is where students over-translate. The register is deliberately plain, and a fluent literary Arabic version of a news lead is a worse translation, not a better one. The graded in-class task falls this week.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Module 5 introduction: general information about media texts (pp. 125–126).
🔍 Read like a sub-editor. Each item is a translation decision in media register. Judge it.
✍️ Write like the genre. Keep it short. If your Arabic is longer than the English, look again.
📝 Week 10 review — 10 questions on media structure, style and categories.

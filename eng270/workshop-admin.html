Administrative Workshop: Guidelines and Regulations

Modals, defined terms, numbering and glossary traps, with examples from and outside the book.

🧭 How this workshop works

This workshop covers Module 6 and Lesson 29 and finishes with new terms and sentences that are not in the book.

  1. Open your book: pp. 161–167 (Lahlali & Abu Hatab, 2014).
  2. Choose a tab, A to F. Together they make seven stages, from checking the reading to whole-sentence translation.
  3. Read the study box at the top of the tab, then answer.
  4. A wrong answer explains why and gives a 📖 pointer to the page. Retry freely; only first-try answers count.
  5. Finish with the 🏆 My score tab: it lists the pages to reread. 63 questions in total.

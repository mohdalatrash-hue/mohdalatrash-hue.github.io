Week 12 workshop · Practical session · administrative texts

The Language of Rules

Company regulations, job descriptions and the documents of governing bodies — a register so restricted that it becomes, unexpectedly, the most tractable on the course.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Formality restricts style, and restricted style restricts choice. This workshop trains the two habits that make the genre safe: fix your terms first, and read every modal twice.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Module 6 introduction and Lesson 29: Translating guidelines and regulations (pp. 162–167).
🔍 Read the modality. In this genre the modal verb is the content. Decide what each sentence does.
✍️ Terms first, then sentences. Build a short defined-terms list before you translate a word — the Week 9 habit, applied to a new genre.
📝 Week 12 review — 10 questions on administrative register, modality and terminology.

Week 11 workshop · Practical session · translating the media

Words That Take Sides

Conflict, uprisings, elections and disasters — the four places where choosing a word is choosing a position, and where machine translation stops being adequate.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Everything before this week has been about accuracy. This week is about responsibility: the same event, described in two defensible ways, produces two different texts — and the translator decides which.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 25 (pp. 127–131), Lesson 26 (pp. 132–137), Lesson 27 (pp. 138–143), Lesson 28 (pp. 144–150).
🔍 Whose word is it? Each pair is defensible. Say what each choice commits the text to.
✍️ Translate, then justify. For each text, write your version and then one sentence naming the loaded terms and the position your choices take.
📝 Week 11 review — 10 questions on the four media registers and the ethics of terminology.

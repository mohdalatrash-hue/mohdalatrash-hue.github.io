How Free Is Free?

Bound free against loose free, the rule that keeps freedom honest, and the unit that the whole process moves through.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Free translation is where students either find their voice or start inventing. This workshop draws the line between the two, using the textbook’s own test: is the rendering derived from the context, or concluded from it?

📖 Reading → Ghazala (2012), free translation and the process; Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 6 (p. 18) and the Module 1 review (p. 20).
🔍 Bound or loose? For each rendering, decide whether it is derived from the context (bound) or concluded from it (loose) — and whether it is defensible.
✍️ Two proverbs, two methods. For each, produce a bound free rendering and a plain direct rendering, then decide which you would submit and why.
📝 Week 3 review — 10 questions on free translation, its two types, and the process and unit of translation.

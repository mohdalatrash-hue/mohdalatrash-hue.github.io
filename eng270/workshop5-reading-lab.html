Fiction Reading Lab

Forty reading-linked activities. Students retrieve the concepts, diagnose adaptation, defend orientation choices, and translate short fictional passages.

How to work: Complete the stations in order. In Stations 2 and 3, a label alone is not enough: write a reason before revealing the model analysis.
12 cases · 15 minutes

Reading retrieval

Check the exact ideas introduced on pages 24–26.

10 cases · 20 minutes

Adaptation detective

Name what changed, then explain the gain and the loss.

10 cases · 20 minutes

Orientation courtroom

Choose source-oriented or target-oriented. The best answer must fit the stated audience and purpose.

8 tasks · 25–40 minutes

Microfiction translation studio

Translate first. Reveal the notes only after writing a draft and a method statement.

Exit ticket: Write one sentence: “For fiction, I choose an orientation by considering ___, ___, and ___; the main risk is ___.”

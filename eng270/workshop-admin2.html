Administrative Workshop 2: Learning and Teaching Regulations

Theory, the seven clauses of the book text, glossary traps and six translation practice sessions. Wrong answers send you back to the page.

🧭 How this workshop works

This workshop covers Lesson 30: Learning and teaching regulations in seven tabs: theory first, then the book text clause by clause, the glossary and exercises, new examples from outside the book, and finally six translation practice sessions.

  1. Open your book: pp. 168–172 (Lahlali & Abu Hatab, 2014); the module opening on p. 161 gives the theory.
  2. Work through the tabs in order, 1 to 7.
  3. Read the box at the top of each tab, then answer. A wrong answer explains why and gives a 📖 pointer to the page; retry freely, only first-try answers count.
  4. In tab 7, write your own translation first, then open the model and tick the checklist (not scored).
  5. Finish with 🏆 My score: it lists the pages to reread. 84 items in total.

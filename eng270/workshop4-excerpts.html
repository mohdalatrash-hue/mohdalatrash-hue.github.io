Week 4 · Evidence-based practice

Excerpt Detective

Read a real English excerpt and its instructional Arabic translation. Identify the method or problem, then defend your answer with evidence from both texts.

30evidence cases
3workshop stations
60–75minutes of practice
Source and translation note: The English excerpts are short quotations from public-domain works and link to their source texts. The Arabic versions are original instructional renderings prepared for this course—not quotations from published Arabic translations. Some are deliberately adapted or imperfect because students must diagnose them.
1 · CompareNotice what stays and what changes: meaning, effect, setting, structure, style or sound.
2 · Decide + justifyChoose one answer and write at least 12 characters explaining your evidence.
3 · CheckSubmit to see the diagnosis and a model justification. Revise your reasoning if needed.

Station 1 — Which translation method?

Choose faithful, communicative, or adaptation. Base your choice on what the Arabic prioritizes—not on whether you personally prefer it.

Station 2 — What is the main translation problem?

Choose the dominant family: grammatical, lexical, stylistic, or phonological. More than one issue may exist; identify the one the question highlights.

Station 3 — Mixed evidence challenge

Make two linked decisions: identify the method and the most important problem. Then explain how the translator’s method responds—or fails to respond—to that problem.

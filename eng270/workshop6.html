Week 6 workshop · Narrative: discourse and syntax

Cohesion, Word Order, Aspect

Where English subordinates, Arabic coordinates; where English has a perfect tense, Arabic has an adverb. The two technical problems of narrative translation.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Most students’ narrative translations fail on punctuation, pronouns and tense rather than on vocabulary. This workshop drills the three contrasts that cause it.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 8 (pp. 27–30) and Lesson 9 (pp. 31–34). Exercises 8.3, 9.1 and 9.3 are set from these lessons — work them in your own copy.
🔍 Diagnose the Arabic. Each item shows a translation decision. Say whether it follows Arabic norms or imports English ones.
✍️ Narrative under the microscope. Both texts are narration. Watch your conjunctions, your pronouns and your aspect — not your vocabulary.
📝 Week 6 review — 10 questions on cohesion, word order, and tense and aspect.

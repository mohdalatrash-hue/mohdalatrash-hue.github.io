Week 13 workshop · Guidelines and regulations

Consistency Is the Content

Education regulations, culture-specific institutional terms, and the discipline of translating a document rather than a sequence of sentences.

🎯 What this workshop trains

This is the last workshop before the legal weeks, and it rehearses the habit the legal genre will demand absolutely: one concept, one term, every time — across a document you may be translating for days.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 30: Learning and teaching regulations (pp. 168–172) and the Module 6 review (p. 173).
🔍 Institutional terms. Each item is a term whose Arabic rendering is an institutional decision, not a dictionary one.
✍️ A document, not a sentence. Translate the whole extract, then check your own terms against each other before you look at the notes.
📝 Week 13 review — 10 questions on education regulations, institutional terms and document-level consistency.

Tuesday · Lessons 3–5 · pp. 9–17

Part I — Choosing a translation method

A method is the whole-text direction. A procedure is one local move used to solve a sentence-, phrase- or word-level problem.

Summary card 1

Essential terminology

SL · Source LanguageOriginal languageاللغة المصدر
TL · Target LanguageLanguage translated intoاللغة الهدف
ST · Source TextOriginal textالنص المصدر
TT · Target TextCompleted translationالنص الهدف
SC · Source CultureCulture behind the originalثقافة المصدر
TC · Target CultureCulture of the new readersثقافة الجمهور الهدف
English is translated into Arabic. Identify SL, TL, ST and TT.
SL: English. TL: Arabic. ST: the English original. TT: the Arabic translation.
Summary card 2

Method versus procedure

Method: the overall approach for the whole text. Procedure: a smaller decision for a sentence, phrase or word.

المنهج يوجّه ترجمة النص كاملاً، أما الإجراء فهو قرار صغير لمعالجة جملة أو عبارة أو كلمة.

Source idiomIt is raining cats and dogs.
Arabic
إنها تمطر بغزارة.
The whole-text priority may be communicative; the local procedure replaces the impossible literal image with its meaning.

Common mistake: treating one word-level decision as the method of the whole text.

Replacing one culture-specific food with an explanation: method or procedure?
Procedure. It is a local solution; the whole text may still follow any appropriate method.
Summary card 3 · Method 1

Faithful translation

Preserve the writer’s precise contextual meaning while allowing the target language to use its own grammar.

تحافظ الترجمة الأمينة على المعنى الدقيق، لكنها لا تنسخ ترتيب الكلمات الإنجليزية بطريقة غير طبيعية.

Used for

Serious prose, quotations and academic or literary ideas.

Preserve

Meaning, details, contrasts and logical relationships.

May change

Word order and construction required for natural Arabic.

EnglishYou see things and say, “Why?” But I dream things that never were and say, “Why not?”
Faithful Arabic
أنت ترى الأشياء وتقول: «لماذا؟» ولكني أحلم بأشياء لم توجد أبداً وأقول: «لمَ لا؟»
The seeing/dreaming and why/why-not contrasts remain; nothing is culturally replaced.
EnglishThe committee rejected the proposal after a long discussion.
Arabic
رفضت اللجنة المقترح بعد مناقشة طويلة.
The message remains precise while Arabic uses natural verbal order.

Memory rule: faithful translation protects the author’s precise meaning—not the English word order.

Does faithful mean translating every word separately?
No. Mechanical copying can become unfaithful when it creates the wrong meaning or unnatural Arabic.
Summary card 4 · Method 2

Communicative translation

Give target readers the message in a familiar, clear and natural form, aiming for the original effect.

تركّز الترجمة التواصلية على الرسالة ووظيفتها وأثرها في القارئ، حتى لو تغيّرت الكلمات أو الصور.

Used for

Signs, greetings, proverbs, idioms, compliments and dialogue.

Preserve

Message, function, purpose and reader response.

May change

Words, images, formulae and cultural references.

Charity begins at home.
الأقربون أولى بالمعروف.
Different words communicate a comparable social lesson.
Diamonds cut diamonds.
لا يفل الحديد إلا الحديد.
Diamond becomes iron because strength—not the object—is the important association.
Dead end · No exit
طريق غير نافذ.
A driver needs the conventional instruction, not a translation of “dead.”

“At the expense of accuracy” means giving up surface wording to protect the intended message. It does not mean changing facts or inventing ideas.

Memory rule: communicative translation protects message, function and effect.

Why is “ممنوع التدخين” effective for “No smoking”?
It is the settled Arabic notice and immediately performs the same function.
Summary card 5 · Method 3

Adaptation

Transfer a work into the target culture while preserving its essential theme, situation, relationships or function.

يحافظ التكييف على الفكرة الأساسية، لكنه قد يغيّر المكان والأسماء واللهجة والعادات لتناسب ثقافة الجمهور الجديد.

May change

Names, places, currency, food, dialect and customs.

Should remain

Theme, essential plot, character roles and dramatic purpose.

PygmalionHow are all your people down at Selsey?
وكيف أهلك في سرغيتا؟
The English village becomes a Lebanese village and the register shifts; the dramatic interaction remains.

Boundary: changing the plot, relationships, purpose and central message may create a new work rather than an adaptation.

Memory rule: adaptation protects the core work but rebuilds its cultural world.

Should a legal contract’s currency be culturally adapted?
No. A contract requires precise factual and legal meaning. Adaptation is unsuitable.

Wednesday · Ghazala · pp. 17–27 · Chapter Two handout

Part II — Diagnosing translation problems

Name the difficulty, use context as evidence, propose solutions, and justify the strongest one.

Summary card 7

What is a translation problem?

Any difficulty that forces a translator to stop, check, reconsider, research or rewrite before transferring meaning appropriately.

مشكلة الترجمة هي صعوبة تجبر المترجم على التوقف والمراجعة أو البحث أو إعادة الصياغة.

The sentence is usually the largest basic working unit. A word becomes clear through its sentence, and the whole text may be needed when the sentence remains ambiguous.

One sentence can contain several families at once. Classification begins the analysis; it does not replace contextual thinking.

Is every unknown word automatically a translation problem?
No. It may only be a vocabulary gap. A translation problem requires a meaningful choice about interpretation or target expression.
Summary card 8 · Grammatical

When structures do not match

Grammatical problems arise when English and Arabic organize meaning through different structures.

Complex source

The main clause may be delayed.

Different grammar

Arabic can use nominal or verbal sentences.

Different order

English often uses SVO; Arabic verbal sentences often begin with a verb.

Of the three books you recommended, I chose only one.
اخترتُ كتاباً واحداً فقط من الكتب الثلاثة التي أوصيتني بها.
Find the main clause first. Arabic need not copy the fronted English phrase.
The weather is beautiful.
الجو جميل.
Arabic expresses the present relationship with a nominal sentence; no direct equivalent of “is” is needed.

Do not copy English grammar; reproduce what the grammar means and does.

Name four other grammatical areas.
Possible answers: modals, questions, negation, tenses, articles, pronouns, adjectives, conditionals and connectors.
Summary card 9 · Lexical

When words do not travel alone

Lexical problems concern unclear, multiple, fixed, specialized or culturally restricted meanings.

Synonymy · الترادفSimilar words may differ in force, register or context.
Polysemy · تعدد المعنىOne form has several meanings.
Collocation · التلازم اللفظيWords conventionally occur together.
Idiom · تعبير اصطلاحيMeaning is not the sum of the words.
Proverb · مثلA fixed cultural saying or lesson.
Metaphor · استعارةAn image communicates indirect meaning.

Context changes “spring”

Spring begins in March.يبدأ الربيع في مارس.
They drank from a spring.شربوا من نبع.
The watch spring broke.انكسر نابض الساعة.
The cat sprang.قفزت القطة.
pass a law

يقرّ قانوناً

make a decision

يتخذ قراراً

It is a tall order.
إنها مهمة صعبة.
This idiom describes a difficult demand; translating “tall” separately fails.

Never select a dictionary meaning before checking context and normal target-language usage.

Classify “make a decision” and “a sitting duck.”
“Make a decision” is a collocation. “A sitting duck” is an idiom.
Summary card 10 · Stylistic

How the writer chooses to say it

Style carries meaning through register, emphasis, pattern, pace, voice, repetition, irony and punctuation.

FormalityFormal, neutral, informal or colloquial.
FrontingMoving an element forward for emphasis.
ParallelismRepeating structures for balance.
AmbiguityMore than one possible interpretation.
RepetitionCohesion, rhythm, emotion or emphasis.
PunctuationPause, connection, suspense and tone.
Suicide he committed.
الانتحار هو ما ارتكبه.
The marked fronting emphasizes “suicide.” A normal order can preserve the fact but lose the focus.
The decision was announced yesterday.
أُعلن القرار أمس.
The agent remains unmentioned. Changing to active voice would require adding or guessing one.

Two textbook warnings: punctuation is meaningful; and English passive should not automatically become Arabic active.

Ask both: “What does it say?” and “Why did the writer say it this way?”

What may be lost if repetition is removed?
Cohesion, emphasis, rhythm, emotion or characterization. Identify its function before changing it.
Summary card 11 · Phonological

When sound helps carry meaning

Phonological problems involve rhyme, rhythm, repeated sounds, wordplay, memorability and beauty.

تتعلق المشكلات الصوتية بالإيقاع والقافية وتكرار الأصوات وتأثيرها في المعنى وجمال النص.

Click it. Pick it. Get it quick.
انقر، اختر، واستلم بسرعة.
Exact rhyme disappears, but short commands and speed remain. Another advertising brief might prioritize stronger Arabic sound.

Exact meaning, rhyme, rhythm and emotional effect cannot always all be preserved. Choose according to the text’s purpose.

Should rhyme matter equally in a poem and a safety warning?
No. A safety warning prioritizes immediate accurate action; a poem may give sound and rhythm much greater importance.
Summary card 12 · Reusable tool

Diagnose, translate, test, justify

  1. Read the complete source.
  2. State its main message.
  3. Mark unclear units.
  4. Name all problem families.
  5. Identify text, purpose and reader.
  1. Select a method.
  2. Draft the translation.
  3. Test accuracy and naturalness.
  4. Back-translate key choices.
  5. Revise and justify.

Solution checklist

Text type · nearby context · whole text · readership · Arabic structure · logic · social acceptability · normality · frequency · familiarity · understandability · back-translation.

He had to eat his words.
اضطر إلى التراجع عن كلامه.
Logic and normal Arabic usage rule out physically eating words.

Translation is diagnosing a problem, proposing solutions, testing them, and defending the strongest choice.

Can one problem have several acceptable solutions?
Yes. Context, purpose and readership may justify different solutions. A strong translator explains the final choice.

Method versus procedure?
A method guides the whole text; a procedure handles a smaller unit.
What accuracy may communicative translation sacrifice?
Surface wording—not the intended message or function.
Why is “spring” polysemous?

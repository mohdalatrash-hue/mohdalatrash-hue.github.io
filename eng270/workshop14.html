Week 14 workshop · Practical session · legal texts

One Legal System into Another

Legal translation is not translation between languages but between legal systems — which is why a familiar-looking equivalent can be the most dangerous word on the page.

🎯 What this workshop trains

The strictest genre on the course, and the one where a small error has consequences outside the text. This workshop trains you to identify what a passage does before deciding how to render it.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 31: Translating legal texts (pp. 186–193).
🔍 What does this passage do? Directive, informative or expressive — and what follows for your Arabic.
✍️ Precision work. Both texts are directive. Read every modal twice and keep every cross-reference exact.
📝 Week 14 review — 10 questions on legal language, vocabulary types and the functions of legal texts.

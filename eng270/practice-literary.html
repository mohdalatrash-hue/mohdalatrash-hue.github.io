🧭 How this practice bank works

You asked for more examples, so this page is pure practice. There are five levels: 1 Recognise the idea, 2 Choose the best translation, 3 Fix a faulty translation, 4 Mixed challenge, and 5 Practice sessions where you translate first and then compare with a model. Pick an answer; if it is wrong you see why and a 📖 pointer to the textbook page. You may retry, but only first-try answers count. Examples marked outside the book are written by the instructor for practice.

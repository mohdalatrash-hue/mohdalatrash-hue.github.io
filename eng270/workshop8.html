Week 8 workshop · Practical session · scientific texts

Terms You Do Not Have

What to do when Arabic has no word yet: borrowing in three forms, coinage in three forms, and the analysis that has to happen before either.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Scientific translation fails on terminology, not on grammar. This workshop gives you the six named ways of filling a lexical gap and the habit of analysing before translating.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 19: Translation strategies (pp. 90–93) and Lesson 20: Translation problems and practical steps (pp. 94–97).
🔍 Which strategy, and would you keep it? Some of these are the standard rendering; some are defensible but risky.
✍️ Terminology before prose. For each text, build a short term list first — source term, chosen Arabic, strategy used — and only then translate.
📝 Week 8 review — 10 questions on borrowing, coinage and the practical steps of scientific translation.

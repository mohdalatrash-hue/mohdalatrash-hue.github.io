Conflict, revolutions, elections and disasters: register, balance and terms, with feedback from the text.

🧭 How this workshop works

This workshop covers Module 5. The first tab is the module opening (what makes a text a media text); the next four tabs are the lessons, each in four stages, ending with a mixed challenge.

  1. Open your book: pp. 125–160 (Lahlali & Abu Hatab, 2014).
  2. Choose a tab: the module opening first, then one tab per lesson (four stages each), then a mixed challenge.
  3. Read the study box at the top of the tab, then answer.
  4. A wrong answer explains why and gives a 📖 pointer to the page. Retry freely; only first-try answers count.
  5. Finish with the 🏆 My score tab: it lists the pages to reread. 125 questions in total.

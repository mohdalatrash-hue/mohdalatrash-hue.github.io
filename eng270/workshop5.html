Two Cultures, One Story

Fiction, narration, and the standing decision every literary translator makes before the first sentence: source-oriented or target-oriented?

🎯 What this workshop trains

Literary translation is the hard case because it works across two cultures rather than two languages. This workshop makes the orientation decision explicit — and gets you translating the Dickens extract you will meet in class.

📖 Reading → Lahlali & Abu Hatab, Lesson 7: Fiction (pp. 24–26).

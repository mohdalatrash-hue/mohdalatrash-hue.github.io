Week 1 workshop · Introducing translation

Meaning, Not Words

Fix the vocabulary of the course, decide what actually crosses from English into Arabic, and learn to name the four routes a word can take.

🎯 What this workshop trains

Every later week assumes you can say what translation transfers and name the route a given rendering took. This workshop makes both automatic, so that in Week 9 you can say ‘that is naturalisation’ without stopping to think.

📖 Reading → Ghazala (2012), opening chapter, and the Chapter One handout. Definition of translation, what we translate, and the process / product / field distinction.
🔍 Name the route. Each Arabic item below reached the language by one of the four routes: a ready equivalent, a coined term, transliteration, or naturalisation. Decide which.
✍️ Two short tasks. Both are ordinary sentences. The point is not difficulty — it is noticing that you have already made three or four decisions before you finish the first clause.
📝 Week 1 review — 10 questions. Definitions, the abbreviations, the four routes, and the three senses of the word translation. Answer order is shuffled on every attempt.

What this course is

ENG 270 acquaints you with the basics of translation: what translation actually means, what its types are, and which methods and techniques translators use. A brief historical account frames the concept, so that translation stops being a vague idea and becomes something you can name and describe — as a process, as a product, and as a field of study. From there the course introduces the main translation genres, each with its own characteristics and its own difficulties, and builds your ability to render a range of text types from English into Arabic using the appropriate tools and resources. Most of the semester is practical: literary, scientific, media, administrative and legal texts, worked through in class.

Course learning outcomes

From the official V2025 course specification.

  • 1.1 · K2Demonstrate a good understanding of the basics of translation as a field, a process and a product.
  • 2.1 · S2Apply translation methods and techniques appropriately and correctly to different texts.
  • 2.2Use translators’ resources to support the translation process.
  • 3.1 · V1Follow professional translation ethics, values and standards, including translation quality assurance.
  • 3.2 · V3Work effectively and constructively in teams.

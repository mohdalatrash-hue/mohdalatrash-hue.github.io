Catch-up Workshop: Scientific Texts, Level by Level

Four lessons, one workshop: grammar gaps, words, collocations and idioms, cohesion. Every wrong answer sends you back to the page.

🧭 How this workshop works

This workshop covers the four catch-up lessons. Each lesson has four stages: check the reading, apply it, the exercises in your book, and beyond the book (new examples that are not in the textbook).

  1. Open your book: pp. 98–124 (Lahlali & Abu Hatab, 2014).
  2. Choose a tab. Each tab is one lesson, built in four stages, and the last tab mixes all four.
  3. Read the study box at the top of the tab, then answer.
  4. A wrong answer explains why and gives a 📖 pointer to the page. Retry freely; only first-try answers count.
  5. Finish with the 🏆 My score tab: it lists the pages to reread. 126 questions in total.

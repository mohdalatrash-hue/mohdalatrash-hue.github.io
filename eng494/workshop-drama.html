From Page to Stage

When does a performable Arabic version remain a translation—and when does it become an adaptation? Experience the problem, read Landers, then rehearse, perform and defend your decisions.

One question across three days

How do we preserve a play’s dramatic effect while making its language natural, speakable and honest for a new audience?

You will work as translator, actor, director, dramaturg, critic and audience. Your writing stays in this browser unless you choose to print it.

01

Adaptation Detective

10 min · individual → pair

Choose the most defensible label. Do not worry about Landers’s terminology yet—record your current judgment. You will revisit these cases Tuesday.

A Song of Ice and Fire novels → HBO’s Game of Thrones

A change of medium transforms narration, structure, pacing and representation: this is adaptation.

English television dialogue → Arabic subtitles

Subtitling is constrained translation. Some local adaptation may occur inside it, but the production itself is not remade.

The Office → Saudi المكتب

The Arabic production was made through an official format relationship and relocates the programme for a Saudi audience.

Ted Lasso compared with فرسان قريح

A shared football-comedy premise does not prove adaptation. Shahid presents فرسان قريح as an original; label only what the evidence supports.

03

One Scene, Four Commissions

17 min · groups of four
Teacher-written scene
MAYA: You promised the report before the meeting.
OMAR: I promised to try.
MAYA: No. You promised because the director was listening.
OMAR: Then perhaps he heard what he wanted to hear.

Translate the exchange four times. The source stays the same; the purpose changes.

Printed literary translation

Preserve textual detail and the controlled tension.

Arabic subtitles

Keep each turn concise enough to read while watching.

Arabic dubbing

Prioritize timing, oral flow and conversational rhythm.

Saudi stage adaptation

Choose a credible local institution, relationship and register.

04

Screen-to-Arabic Case Gallery

18 min · rotate through four stations

Use these cases to separate medium adaptation, translation, format adaptation, localization and unsupported assumptions.

Station A · adaptation chain

Game of Thrones

HBO source ↗

Novel → screen adaptation → Arabic subtitles. What shifts when narration becomes image, actor, music and spoken dialogue? How should invented names, titles and elevated register travel into Arabic?

Station B · humour in subtitles

Ted Lasso

Apple TV source ↗

Arabic subtitles must handle football terminology, British/American references, motivational language and character-specific humour. Which effects can concise subtitles preserve, and which may need compensation?

Station C · evidence test

Ted Lasso and فرسان قريح

Shahid source ↗

Map premise, character functions, tone and plot—but do not convert resemblance into a factual production relationship. What evidence would change your conclusion?

Station D · licensed local version

The Office and المكتب

Production source ↗

Identify the transferable format, then predict what setting, institution, relationships, jokes and dialect must change for a Saudi production—and what must remain recognizable.

05

The Time-Machine Test

12 min · preserve → modernize → adapt
Public-domain dramatic situation · freely paraphrased
A young character must tell a powerful older figure that a carefully arranged marriage will not happen. The older figure believes obedience is public proof of family honour.

Period translation

Retain the historical world and its hierarchy.

Language modernization

Keep the setting, but make the Arabic accessible in 2026.

Contemporary adaptation

Move the conflict into a credible present-day context.

06

First Rehearsal Lab

20 min · translator ↔ actor/director
Teacher-written rehearsal scene
A community-club office. LINA blocks the doorway as SALEM tries to leave.
LINA: If you leave now, they will assume you agreed.
SALEM: They assume that whenever I stop arguing.
LINA: Then do not stop.
SALEM: Easy advice from someone standing near the door.
LINA: I am standing here so you cannot use it.
SALEM: That is either loyalty or a fire-safety violation.

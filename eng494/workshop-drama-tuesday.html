02

Four Directors, One Scene

17 min · select a commission and build its version
Teacher-written capstone scene
Minutes before a public announcement.
HADI: You changed the last page.
NOOR: I corrected it.
HADI: Without telling me.
NOOR: If I had told you, it would still be wrong.
HADI: And if the chair asks?
NOOR: Tell her the truth. Carefully.
Select your commissioning brief.
03

Silent Stage

10 min · perform action before dialogue

Perform the capstone scene once without words—only distance, gesture, interruption, eye contact and movement. Then decide what the Arabic no longer needs to explain.

04

Rehearsal Carousel

20 min · translator · actor · director · dramaturg

Rotate the four roles after the first performance. The director then introduces one interruption: one breath, hidden anger, formal Arabic, a Qassim setting, a final-word laugh, or movement during the line.

05

15 min · listen first, label second

Hear the close and modernized versions without labels. Rate the version your group is evaluating; then reveal how it was produced.

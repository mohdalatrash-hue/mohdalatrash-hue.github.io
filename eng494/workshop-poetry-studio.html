Unit 3 · Adult Poetry · Final practice workshop

Strategy Studio: One Verse, One Strategy

Six famous English poems. Six translation strategies. For each one: read the rules, watch a worked Arabic sample, then translate the next lines yourself using the same strategy.

⏱️ How to work · 40–45 minutes

Do the six stations in order, about 6 minutes each (use the timer on each station). In every station: 1 read the strategy rules, 2 study the worked sample and its notes, 3 translate the “Your turn” lines with the same strategy, 4 tick the self-check, 5 only then open the model answer. Finish with the Final Round (counts in My Progress). Your translations are saved on this device.

Strategies from Lahlali & Abu Hatab (2014, p. 45), following Bassnett-McGuire (1980). One golden rule: do not mix strategies inside a station. A literal translation should look literal; a rhymed one must rhyme.

🏁 Final round · 12 questions · 5 minutes. You see an Arabic rendering; name the strategy. Your score is saved in My Progress.
Last reflection · 2 minutes
Which strategy was hardest for you, and which one would you choose for your Final Project poem? Why?

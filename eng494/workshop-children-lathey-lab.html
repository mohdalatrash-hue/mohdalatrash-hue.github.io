Inside Lathey: Translating Sound

A knowledge check on the required reading (Lathey, 2016, Ch. 5, pp. 93–111). Read a few pages, answer, and get instant feedback with the page to re-read.

📖 How this lab works · about 45–60 minutes

Open the chapter in Blackboard next to this page. Each tab names the pages to read, then asks what Lathey actually says. Every answer gives instant feedback with the page number, so you can check the text. This lab is about the reading; the translation tasks are in the main workshop (Sing It, Then Study It).

This week's content
Unit question: Are first impressions accurate?
We opened the unit by discussing how much we can tell about someone from a first look — their face, clothing, and manner — and previewed the vocabulary we'll need to talk about it.
Note-taking skill: using notes to summarize a lecture
A good way to remember a lecture is to put the key ideas into your own words as soon as possible afterward — the topic, the main idea, and a few important points and examples. We practiced the signpost phrases lecturers use: "the speaker talked about…", "then she explained…", "after that, she wrapped up with…".
Listening 1 preview: Faces and First Impressions
We previewed the key words for this listening (aspect, assume, encounter, familiar, form a first impression, judge, preference, tend to, trait, trust) and set up the topic: psychological research on how faces shape first impressions.

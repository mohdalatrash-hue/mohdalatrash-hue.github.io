What this course is

ENGP 1002 is the second General English course of the Preparatory Year, focused on listening and speaking. The coursebook, Q: Skills for Success (Fourth Edition, Special Edition) Level 3, organizes each of its eight units around a big question — from are first impressions accurate? to units on biological sciences, engineering, business, technology and social sciences — and builds the same skill set every time: a note-taking or listening strategy, two audio-based listening passages with comprehension and inference tasks, a vocabulary strand (often suffixes or collocations), a grammar point tied to speaking, a pronunciation focus, and a final paired discussion assignment that draws the whole unit together.

Teams Speaking Progress — homework policy

These five rules apply to every Teams Speaking Progress submission, all semester.

  • Weekly requirementStudents must complete ONE Speaking Progress assignment per week on Microsoft Teams.
  • Pronunciation & lexical drillsStudents must use the built-in AI rehearsal tool to correct any flagged mispronunciations before submitting their work.
  • Mandatory in-app video modeRecordings must be completed directly inside Teams with the video camera activated. External audio/video file uploads will not receive credit.
  • Camera alignment & eye contactStudents must maintain consistent eye contact with the phone/tablet/webcam lens during recording.
  • Strict anti-AI & scripting policyStudents are strictly prohibited from reading AI-generated scripts (e.g., ChatGPT) or pre-written text. Speech must be natural and spontaneous. Reading pre-written text results in a zero grade.

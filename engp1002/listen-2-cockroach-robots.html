Cockroaches that teach robots

About this exercise

How a cockroach’s body gave engineers ideas for tough, flexible robots. Lots of numbers to catch.

Video length4:54
LevelMedium
Note formatT-chart + numbers
Time needed40–50 minutes

Fits the Unit 3 theme nature and innovation. Unit 3 skills: taking notes about events (see the robot-making steps) and listening for time markers. This is extra practice: it uses a real talk and its own vocabulary, so it does not replace the textbook activities or the Teams homework.

Routine: 1 Predict → 2 Watch once (main idea, few notes) → 3 Watch again and take notes → 4 Check with a partner → 5 Answer the questions → 6 Summarise from your notes → 7 Speak.

1Before you watch

Key words

robuststrong and able to keep working in difficult conditions
terrainthe type of ground, such as rough or smooth ground
self-stabilizeto stop yourself from falling without outside help
maneuvera skilled movement, such as a turn or a jump
exoskeletona hard skeleton on the outside of an insect’s body
compliantflexible; able to bend
gapa narrow space between two things
withstandto survive a strong force without breaking

These are extension words for this video. They are not from the textbook word list.

Before you watch: how could an insect help an engineer build a better robot? Write two ideas.

4Check your understanding

Comprehension questions

Listen for numbers

Type the number you hear (digits only).

1.Cockroaches can flip over and disappear in less than milliseconds.

2.A robot can be made in less than minutes.

3.They can go through gaps that are millimetres high.

4.They can compress their bodies by more than percent.

5.They can withstand forces times their body weight.

⭐ About the Placement Test

The placement test is diagnostic and carries no marks. It does not appear in Blackboard and it does not affect your grade in any way. Its only purpose is to show you and me what you already recognise, so the rest of the semester can be pitched properly. Do not revise for it — an honest result is a useful result.

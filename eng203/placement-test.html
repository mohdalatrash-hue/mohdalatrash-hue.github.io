Thirty questions across medicine, computer science, physics, the general sciences and word formation. This test is not graded and carries no marks.

⚠️ Read this before you begin — this test is NOT graded

Nothing here counts towards your grade in ENG 203. The test has two purposes only: it shows Dr. Alatrash where the class as a whole is starting from, so the course can be pitched correctly; and it shows you your own baseline, so you can see later in the semester how far you have moved. There is no pass mark and no failure. Guessing wildly only gives you a misleading picture of yourself — answer honestly, and leave the ones you truly do not know to chance rather than to a friend.
